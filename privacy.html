Brainrot Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Brainrot ("Brainrot", "we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our services.

1. Information We Collect

When you connect supported social media accounts using OAuth, Brainrot may receive information required to authenticate your account and provide the requested functionality.

This information may include:

  • Public profile information.
  • Account identifiers.
  • OAuth access tokens and authorization information.
  • Media files that you choose to process or publish.
  • Information required to operate authorized automation workflows.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information only for legitimate purposes related to providing and operating Brainrot, including:

  • Authenticating connected accounts.
  • Executing actions that you explicitly authorize.
  • Creating, processing, or publishing content requested by you.
  • Maintaining and improving service reliability.
  • Protecting the security of the service.

3. Third-Party Platform Access

Brainrot may integrate with third-party platforms such as TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, Google, and other supported services.

When you authorize Brainrot to access a third-party platform, Brainrot uses the permissions granted by you for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

Your use of third-party platforms remains subject to their respective privacy policies and terms of service.

4. Data Storage

Certain information, including authorization information and workflow data, may be stored using infrastructure required to operate the service.

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect stored information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure.

5. Data Sharing

Brainrot does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Information may only be shared with service providers when necessary to provide functionality that you have requested, to operate the service, or when required by law.

6. Data Deletion

You may revoke access to connected third-party accounts at any time through the relevant platform.

You may also contact us to request deletion of information associated with your use of Brainrot.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect information handled by Brainrot. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

Brainrot is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will become effective when the updated Privacy Policy is published on this page.
